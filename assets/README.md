# Assets

| Folder | What | Ink / use |
|---|---|---|
| `logos/v5/` | The v5 interlocking-brackets mark: `cofferdam-mark-v5.svg` (light tones, on Cerulean), `cofferdam-mark-v5-on-dark.svg` (dark tones), `cofferdam-mark-v5-white.svg` (single ink), four piece layers, icon previews | Light tones never on white |

| `partners/self/` | Self.xyz logo (on black / on white) | Never restyle |

The full Base Brand Guidelines PDF stays in `cofferdam-app/assets/base/`.

`../motion/splash/` holds the launch splash (Expo source, timings, preview video and keyframes). The v5 SVGs keep the designer's geometry byte for byte; their fills were retoned on 2026-10-10 to the tightened palette (`mark-*` tokens), so their original C2PA manifests were removed. `preview-*.png`, the splash videos and keyframes are re-rendered in the current tones by `scripts/render-previews.py`; the designer's renders are kept in `original-renders/`. `lockups/` and the black and mono marks are generated by `scripts/make-logos.py`.
